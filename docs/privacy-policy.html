Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: August 2026 📍 Roorkee, Uttarakhand, India 📱 Al Habibi Feast App
Summary: We only collect what we need to deliver your order — your name, phone, and address. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.
📋

Information We Collect

When you place an order through the Al Habibi Feast app, we collect only what is necessary to fulfill your order:

Data Type Why We Collect It Shared? Required?
Full Name Order identification and delivery No Yes
Phone Number Order confirmation and delivery contact Rider only Yes
Delivery Address To complete your delivery Rider only Yes
PIN Code Confirm serviceability (Roorkee 247667) No Yes
Order Details Items, quantities, and total amount No Yes
We do not collect payment card details, passwords, government IDs, or location/GPS data.
🎯

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your food orders
  • To contact you about your order status via WhatsApp or phone call
  • To coordinate delivery with our delivery riders
  • To improve our menu and service quality
🔒

Data Sharing & Security

Your delivery address and phone number are shared only with our delivery riders to complete your order. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, analytics companies, or any unrelated third parties.

All data transmitted between the app and our server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Your order information is stored on our secure server at alhabibifeast.in.

🗑️

Data Deletion & Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data at any time. We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.

To request deletion of your data, visit our dedicated page:

Request Data Deletion →

We retain order data for up to 12 months for customer support and legal compliance, after which it is permanently deleted.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
