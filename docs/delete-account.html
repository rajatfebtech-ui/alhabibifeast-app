Request Account & Data Deletion

If you would like to delete your account and all associated personal data from Al Habibi Feast, you can submit a deletion request using any of the methods below.

Option 1: Email Us

Send an email from your registered phone/email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Send Email Request

Option 2: WhatsApp

Send us a WhatsApp message with the text "Delete my data" along with your registered phone number.

WhatsApp Us

What data will be deleted?

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your delivery addresses
  • Your order history

How long does it take?

We will process your deletion request within 7 business days and confirm via WhatsApp or email.
